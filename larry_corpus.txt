--- SYSTEM LOG / ENTRY: 00002 The Artist.txt ---

I was staring at a gorgeous Texas sky line today when this thought occurred to me
It was one of those slowly rolling hot Texas summer days 
Far away from the the sterile mechanized feeling that the scenery of a large city can give you
I was surrounded by mostly wide open farm land where the sky was bright blue
And big vibrant white fluffy clouds lazily meandered their way across the sky
Like a herd animal as it slowly grazed its pasture

I was driving a winding back road
On my way home from some tedious errand I had to do for the day
I drove with my window down
I drove with a light cool breeze blowing across my face
I drove in silence

Moments like these are often times the best time for me to reflect
I was comfortable in my bright red Mustang
Cruising down a familiar windy little back road
And at peace enjoying the serenity of the moment

That moment of clarity led me to this
We are artists creating art pieces for a gallery that we all share and know as life

Imagine this experience that we collectively call life as a painting that you are creating
When we are born we have nothing but ourselves
THE ARTIST
We don't know what we are supposed to create or how to even create it
We have no canvas, paper, or any other media to paint on
We have no supplies, no brushes, no paints
Not even water colors or a pencil for an outline

We simply have an empty artist
The most blank of all canvas

Our parents and those closest to us who raise us are the first ones to begin gifting us
Helping us with the supplies, techniques, and inspiration that we need to create
We grow and begin to understand 
Or at least start to get an idea
An idea of what type of picture we are painting
As we continue creating our art and time goes on we accumulate new supplies
Learn new techniques
And gain new inspiration for the piece we are eternally crafting

We can acquire all shades of colors imaginable
Different types of brushes
Different painting techniques
Different type of media to work in
Some of these are gifts from other artists we meet along the way
And some of them we may have found on our own

At some points in time in our life there may be others
Others who attempt to shape our painting or even do it for us
This is especially true during our childhood and developmental times in life
Our parents can begin our painting for us 
But the glory of this artwork is that ultimately
This painting is for you
YOU ARE THE ARTIST
And you alone have the right to choose what supplies and colors to use to paint for yourself


Some choose to paint a picture full of darkness
They bury their time in this experience of life focusing on all the negative
Negatives that they can recreate in their painting in all its various shades of dark colors 
And even darker imagery
For some reason their painting needs it
These types of souls can thrive off negative human experiences
Experiences such as pain, fear, anxiety, sadness, and depression
They need it for inspiration for their painting

Some choose to paint a picture full of vibrant colors
Using avant garde painting methods and techniques
They focus their art on the bright colors
They focus their art on positive experiences in life

Their art needs it and so do we
All the other artists
We need both of these types of artists
They are a constant reminder to us all that only through balance 
Can we create true beauty

Some artists are reckless and brash
The kind of artist that paints in bold permanent strokes
Often times flinging the paint across whatever odd media they had chosen as a canvas
I'm sure we have all met a few in our life times
The kind of person who comes into your life 
Like a comet streaking brilliantly across the night sky
In an awe inspiring majestic glow before it crashes into the ground
Often times these artists sadly take their own painting with them
Lost forever to all 
All but those who still had some memory of the piece they had created

Some artists choose to create only one painting
They are so sure of themselves
So sure of everything they had captured in their single attempt
So sure that they feel no need to paint again
So they leave their painting behind in the gallery
So that all us other artists can gaze at it for inspiration

Some choose to paint the same picture
The same picture over and over again
This isn't their first painting
And it won't be their last
They have painted the same picture so many times
They have forgotten when they even began painting it
Or why...
This is what they know and what they are comfortable with
So comfortable that when the given the opportunity to do so again
They will paint the exact same picture without even knowing it

This is ok for a lot of the artists out there
It may be ok for most even
Black and white images
Simple geometric shapes 
These are the basis for all art
However complex and beautiful it may be

These kinds of sketches are simple and easy to create
But at the end of it all
When your painting is finished
You are the final art critic
And some choose to see the beauty of it all
The beauty in those simple geometric shapes
The beauty in the black and white colors

I AM PAINTING MY MASTERPIECE
My hope is that every last one of my readers is painting their own at this moment as well
I feel on some level like I have attempted this painting before
It did not quite come out the way I wanted
Somehow I needed more darkness to properly contrast the light
Maybe I didn't quite have the right shades of colors I needed

I feel driven
Compelled even
To use every method
Every color I can find
To get this painting just right
I have driven myself to the brink of insanity
Walked through the darkness past to the other side
Just to acquire all the paints, brushes, and supplies I may need

My hope is that through reading this
I might gift to you that last shade of paint
Help guide your hand for the brush stroke
That one brush stroke you might need to finish your own masterpiece
